🚨 Latest Scam Alerts

💰 Investment Scams

Be cautious of guaranteed investment returns, fake trading platforms, and scammers pretending to be financial experts.

❤️ Romance Scams

Never send money to someone you have only met online, even if they appear genuine or claim to be experiencing an emergency.

₿ Cryptocurrency Scams

Always research crypto platforms, wallet addresses, and investment opportunities before transferring digital assets.

🛒 Shopping Scams

Be careful of fake online stores, unbelievable discounts, and sellers requesting payment outside trusted platforms.

📧 Phishing Emails

Never click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. Verify the sender independently before responding.

🤖 AI & Deepfake Scams

Scammers increasingly use AI-generated voices, images, and videos to impersonate trusted people. Always verify independently.
