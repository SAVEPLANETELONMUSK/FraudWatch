Common Scam Categories

💰 Investment Scams

Be cautious of guaranteed returns, urgent investment opportunities and requests to send funds through unusual payment channels.

₿ Crypto Scams

Watch for fake exchanges, impersonation, fraudulent investment platforms and requests for cryptocurrency payments.

💼 Fake Job Scams

Be careful when someone asks you to pay fees, purchase equipment or provide sensitive information before employment.

❤️ Romance Scams

Be cautious when an online relationship quickly leads to requests for money, gifts, transfers or financial assistance.

🎣 Phishing

Suspicious links, fake login pages and urgent messages can be used to steal passwords or other sensitive information.

🛡 Impersonation

Scammers may pretend to be banks, companies, government officials, celebrities, friends or family members.

📦 Shopping Scams

Watch for unusually low prices, pressure to pay outside trusted platforms and sellers who disappear after payment.

⚠️ Recovery Scams

Be extremely cautious of anyone promising guaranteed recovery of previously lost funds in exchange for an upfront payment.

⚠️ Important Information

A reported indicator is not automatically proof that a person, business, website or account is fraudulent. FraudWatch uses reported information and public-source indicators for awareness and educational purposes.

Always consider the available evidence and verify important information through appropriate official or trusted sources.
