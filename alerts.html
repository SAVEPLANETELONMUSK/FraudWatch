SCAM ALERT

🚨 Scam of the Week: Fake Investment Opportunities

Fraudsters may use social media, messaging apps, websites or personal introductions to promote investment opportunities that promise unusually high or guaranteed returns.

⚠️ Warning signs: Guaranteed profits, pressure to act quickly, requests to send money to personal accounts or cryptocurrency wallets, and instructions to move conversations away from trusted platforms.

Before sending money, independently verify the company, investment professional and payment destination using appropriate official sources.

⚠️ Red Flags to Watch

💸 Guaranteed Returns

Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed profits or unusually high returns with little or no risk.

⏰ Urgent Pressure

Scammers often create urgency so you make a payment before you have time to verify the opportunity.

🔐 Sensitive Information

Never provide passwords, PINs, one-time codes, recovery phrases or private keys to someone claiming to help you.

💳 Unusual Payments

Be careful when someone insists on cryptocurrency, gift cards, wire transfers or other difficult-to-reverse payments.

🎭 Impersonation

Verify unexpected requests from banks, companies, officials, celebrities, friends or family through a trusted channel.

🆘 Recovery Promises

Anyone promising guaranteed recovery of lost funds for an upfront fee deserves careful scrutiny.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

1. Stop

Don't send additional money or information while you investigate a suspicious request.

2. Verify

Contact the organization through an independently verified website, phone number or official channel.

3. Preserve Evidence

Keep messages, emails, receipts, transaction records, screenshots, URLs and account details.

4. Report

Report suspected fraud to the appropriate platform, financial institution and relevant authorities.

📢 Think You've Encountered a Scam?

Don't panic and don't send more money. Preserve your evidence and report what happened so the information can be reviewed appropriately.
