🧾 Evidence & Investigation

Useful Evidence

  • 📸 Screenshots of suspicious messages or websites
  • 🌐 Website URLs and domains
  • 📧 Email addresses and suspicious messages
  • 📞 Phone numbers used during contact
  • 💬 Telegram, WhatsApp or social-media usernames
  • 💳 Transaction references and payment records
  • ₿ Public cryptocurrency wallet addresses
  • 🕒 Dates, times and a clear incident timeline

Public Information Review

FraudWatch may help organise publicly available information relevant to a reported incident, such as domains, usernames, public business information and other publicly accessible indicators.

Public information is an investigative lead, not proof that a person or organisation committed fraud.

💰 Recovery Assistance

If you have lost money, FraudWatch can help you organise the information needed to contact your bank, payment provider, platform, cryptocurrency service or appropriate authorities.

Recovery outcomes depend on the circumstances, payment method, timing and actions of the relevant financial institution or authority. FraudWatch does not guarantee that funds will be recovered.

🛡️ Investigation Safety

FraudWatch focuses on lawful investigation, evidence preservation and public-information research.

Never attempt to hack an account, access private systems, threaten someone, publish private personal information or confront an alleged scammer.

Never submit passwords, PINs, OTP codes, cryptocurrency seed phrases, private keys or other account credentials.

Need Help?

If you believe you have been targeted by fraud, preserve your evidence and report the incident as soon as possible.
