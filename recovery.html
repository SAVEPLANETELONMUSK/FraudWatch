Recovery of Funds

If you believe you have lost money or digital assets through fraud, acting quickly can help protect your accounts and preserve important evidence. Follow these practical steps and use legitimate recovery channels.

Recovery of Funds: What To Do

1. Stop Further Payments

Do not send additional money, even if someone promises to recover your funds or asks for an upfront recovery fee.

2. Contact Your Bank or Payment Provider

Notify your bank, card issuer, payment provider, cryptocurrency exchange, or other relevant financial service as soon as possible.

3. Preserve Your Evidence

Save messages, emails, receipts, transaction records, screenshots, wallet addresses, usernames, website addresses, and other information connected to the incident.

4. Secure Your Accounts

Change compromised passwords, enable two-factor authentication, review account activity, and secure any accounts that may have been exposed.

5. Report the Fraud

Report the incident to the appropriate financial institution, platform, law-enforcement agency, regulator, or other relevant authority in your jurisdiction.

6. Be Careful of Recovery Scams

Fraud victims are often targeted again by people claiming they can recover lost funds. Be cautious of guaranteed recovery promises, upfront fees, pressure, or requests for sensitive credentials.

Important Recovery Notice

FraudWatch does not guarantee that lost funds will be recovered. Recovery depends on factors such as the payment method, timing, available evidence, financial institutions, platforms, authorities, and the circumstances of each case.

Never provide passwords, banking PINs, one-time verification codes, recovery phrases, private keys, or other highly sensitive credentials to anyone claiming to assist with recovery.
